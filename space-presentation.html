Vocab Unit 5 Set A – Words from the Void

Arbitrary
(Adjective)
unreasonable; based on one's wishes or whims without regard for reason or fairness
Syn: capricious, high-handed, autocratic
Ant: reasoned, rational, objective, equitable
Ex: A judge may be criticized for rulings that appear to be arbitrary and without legal precedent.
Catalyst
(Noun)
a substance that causes or hastens a chemical reaction; any agent that causes change
Syn: stimulus, spur, instigator
Ex: The bombing attack was the catalyst for war.
Facilitate
(Verb)
to make easier, to assist
Syn: ease, smooth the way, simplify
Ant: hamper, hinder, obstruct, impede
Ex: Her rise to power was facilitated by her influential friends.
Incorrigible
(Adjective)
not able to be corrected; beyond control
Syn: unruly, intractable, incurable, inveterate
Ant: tractable, docile, curable, reparable
Ex: He is always the class clown, and his teachers say he is incorrigible.
Militant
(Adjective / Noun)
given to fighting; active and aggressive in support of a cause; (n.) an activist
Syn: truculent
Ant: unassertive, peaceable, passive
Ex: Martin Luther King, Jr., advocated peaceful rather than militant protest.
Paramount
(Adjective)
chief in importance, above all others
Syn: supreme, primary, dominant
Ant: secondary, subordinate, ancillary
Ex: The safety of the passengers is paramount to the airline industry.
Rebut
(Verb)
to offer arguments or evidence that contradict an assertion; to refute
Syn: disprove, confute, shoot holes in
Ant: confirm, corroborate, substantiate
Ex: He was able to rebut the accusations made against him with evidence of his innocence.
Reprimand
(Verb / Noun)
to scold; find fault with; (n.) a rebuke
Syn: reproach, reproof
Ant: pat on the back
Ex: When I misbehaved in school, my teacher sent me home with a written reprimand for my parents to sign.
Servitude
(Noun)
slavery, forced labor
Syn: bondage, thralldom
Ant: liberty
Ex: In the past, attempts to commit murder were punishable by penal servitude for life.
Slapdash
(Adjective)
careless and hasty
Syn: cursory, perfunctory, slipshod
Ant: painstaking, thorough, in-depth
Ex: Her slapdash approach to studying resulted in a poor grade on the exam.
