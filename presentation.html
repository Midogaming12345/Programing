Who is the Spy?

Game Description: A thrilling bluffing game for 14 players. 13 players secretly know the word (they're "in"); 1 is the spy and doesn't know it. Players ask yes/no questions in a chain to deduce who the spy is. The spy wins by guessing the word; others win by voting out the spy. Play multiple rounds with random words!

  • 1. Randomly select a word from the 10 (e.g., roll a die: 1=Abscond, 2=Access, etc.).
  • 2. Assign roles on the next slide.
  • 3. Questions: Chain them (Player 1 asks Player 2, 2 asks 3, etc.). Must relate to the word.
  • 4. After 10-15 questions, move to voting.
  • 5. Spy guesses the word at the end.

Sample Question Chain (for word "Intrepid"): Player 1 asks Player 2: "Is it related to bravery?" (Yes.) Player 2 asks Player 3: "Does it mean fearless?" (Yes.) And so on—the spy bluffs!
