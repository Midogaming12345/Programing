Abscond

Definition: To leave hurriedly and secretly, typically to avoid detection.

Example: The prisoner absconded from the jail under cover of night.

Access

Definition: The means or opportunity to approach or enter a place.

Example: She used her keycard to gain access to the secure building.

Arduous

Definition: Involving or requiring strenuous effort; difficult.

Example: The arduous hike up the mountain left us exhausted.

Disentangle

Definition: To separate or free something from a tangle.

Example: He had to disentangle the fishing line from the weeds.

Fated

Definition: Determined by fate; destined.

Example: It seemed fated that they would meet again.

Intrepid

Definition: Fearless and adventurous.

Example: The intrepid journalist traveled to war zones to report the truth.

Pompous

Definition: Affectedly and irritatingly grand, solemn, or self-important.

Example: His pompous speech made everyone roll their eyes.

Precipice

Definition: A very steep rock face or cliff, typically a tall one.

Example: They stood on the precipice, gazing at the valley below.

Reprieve

Definition: A cancellation or postponement of a punishment.

Example: The storm gave us a reprieve from the heatwave.

Revile

Definition: To criticize in an abusive or angrily insulting manner.

Example: The fans reviled the referee for the bad call.

Who is the Spy?

Game Description: A thrilling bluffing game for 14 players. 13 players secretly know the word (they're "in"); 1 is the spy and doesn't know it. Players ask yes/no questions in a chain to deduce who the spy is. The spy wins by guessing the word; others win by voting out the spy. Play multiple rounds with random words!

Rules:

  • Randomly select a word from the 10 (e.g., roll a die: 1=Abscond, 2=Access, etc.).
  • Assign roles on the next slide.
  • Questions: Chain them (Player 1 asks Player 2, 2 asks 3, etc.). Must relate to the word.
  • After 10-15 questions, move to voting.
  • Spy guesses the word at the end.
  • Sample Question Chain (for word "Intrepid"): Player 1 asks Player 2: "Is it related to bravery?" (Yes.) Player 2 asks Player 3: "Does it mean fearless?" (Yes.) And so on—the spy bluffs!
