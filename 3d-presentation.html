🕵️Word Spy Game

This is a fun word-guessing game called "Word Spy" (inspired by games like "Who is the Spy?"). There are 7 players. 6 players secretly know one of the words from this presentation (e.g., "disentangle" – you can choose or randomize it). The 7th player (the "spy") doesn't know the word and must bluff to avoid being caught.

📋Rules

  • Players 1-7 sit in a circle.
  • The game master assigns the word to 6 players and tells them it's a secret. The 7th doesn't get it.
  • Questions go in a chain: Player 2 asks Player 3, Player 3 asks Player 1, and so on (looping back). Each question must relate to the word (e.g., "Is it an animal?").
  • After 10 questions, vote on who the spy is.
  • The spy wins if they guess the word correctly at the end; others win if they identify the spy.

🎬Sample Script

Setup: Player 1 is the spy. The word is "cat".

Round 1: Player 2 asks Player 3: "Does it have fur?"
Player 3 (knowing it's "cat"): "Yes."
Round 2: Player 3 asks Player 1: "Is it something you can pet?"
Player 1 (as spy, trying to blend in): "Yes."
Round 3: Player 1 asks Player 4: "Does it live in a house?"
Player 4: "Yes."
Round 4: Player 4 asks Player 5: "Is it bigger than a dog?"
Player 5: "No."
Round 5: Player 5 asks Player 6: "Does it meow?"
Player 6: "Yes."
Round 6: Player 6 asks Player 7: "Is it a cat?"
Player 7: "Yes."
Round 7: Player 7 asks Player 2: "Can it climb trees?"
Player 2: "Yes."

Continue the chain for more rounds. The spy might slip up by giving wrong answers!

End the game by revealing the word and spy. Play multiple rounds with different words!
